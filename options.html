What the Keys dialog shows and how long the keys last. Keys themselves are never stored anywhere.

Session length

A role allows at most its own maximum session duration. When AWS refuses the length asked for, the add-on retries at 1 hour, then 15 minutes, and says so in the dialog.

Privacy

The add-on reads the SAML response on AWS's sign-in page, sends it with the chosen role to AWS STS (AssumeRoleWithSAML) and shows what STS returns. Nothing is stored, logged or sent anywhere else. Full policy: PRIVACY.md.
